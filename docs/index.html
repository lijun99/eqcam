eqcam

Rapid survey of public and traffic camera videos after an earthquake with vision-language models.

Demo mapA made-up M6.8 event with synthetic videos and made-up findings of every severity: the filter by tag, the popups, the tiles. Event surveysReal events surveyed with eqcam: survey maps, vibration maps, tables. Source and documentationThe code, the README (install, inputs, backends, outputs) and the Claude Code skill.

What it does

  • prepare: finds when the shaking reaches each camera (its vibration, within one view, not the camera panning or switching views), picks the frames before, during and after, and flags cameras that go dark, freeze, move, or whose mount resonates.
  • review: a vision-language model (Claude, Gemini or Qwen) looks at each camera's frames for damage, landslides, ground failure, fire, smoke, outages, water and people's response, and estimates the shaking intensity (MMI) from the visible effects; it also boxes the people, who are blurred before anything is published.
  • report: a table and a map, the cameras colored by severity, with the video linked at the moment of the detections; optionally (--vibration) the vibration of each camera and of the objects in its view: displacement, spectra, spectrogram.

Research prototype. The findings are automated and unverified, and the image motion of a camera is not a measurement of the ground shaking; nothing here is an official earthquake product or meant for emergency decisions.
